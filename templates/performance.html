Machine Learning Metric Glossary

Plain-English explanations for evaluators

Recall measures what percentage of all real fraudulent transactions the model successfully detected. In credit card fraud, high recall is vital because a missed fraud (False Negative) results in direct financial loss and chargeback fees.

Precision indicates what percentage of flagged fraud alerts were actually fraudulent. Higher precision prevents false card declines, reducing customer frustration and friction at checkouts.

The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. Unlike simple arithmetic mean, F1 punishes extreme imbalances between Precision and Recall, making it an indispensable measure of balanced model utility.

The area under the Precision-Recall curve. On heavily skewed datasets (where negative cases dominate), ROC-AUC can present an overly optimistic score because the True Negative count is huge. PR-AUC focuses exclusively on the positive minority class and is considered the gold standard benchmark for fraud detection.
